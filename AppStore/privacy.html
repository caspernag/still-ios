Still

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: Still does not collect any data. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Still is made by Nag Software, Bergen, Norway. Contact: casper@nagsoftware.no.

Screen Time

Still uses Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) to lock the apps, categories and websites you choose. When you choose apps, iOS gives Still only opaque tokens. Still cannot see which apps are installed, which apps you use or for how long, and it never sees your browsing.

What is stored, and where

To work, Still keeps the following on your device only, in its private app storage:

  • Your selection of apps, categories and websites (as Apple's opaque tokens)
  • Your sessions: start and end times, and how many times you tapped "Close" on the lock screen
  • Your schedules and settings

This data never leaves your iPhone and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting Still deletes it. It may be included in your own encrypted device backup, which Apple manages.

Purchases

Still Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Still schedules a local notification on your device when a session ends. No push service is used.

What we don't do

  • No tracking and no advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No sharing or selling of data

Children

Still is a self-control tool for your own device and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
