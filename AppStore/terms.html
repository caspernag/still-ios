The app

Still, made by Nag Software (Bergen, Norway), helps you lock distracting apps for a time you choose, using Apple's Screen Time framework. Phone, Messages and other apps iOS always allows keep working during a session.

Still Pro subscription

  • Still Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, offered yearly and monthly. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may vary by country.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. When the trial ends, the subscription price is charged to your Apple Account unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price and length unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription anytime in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or from Still → Settings → Manage.
  • Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

Your responsibility

Still is a self-control tool, not a parental-control or security product. Strict mode is designed so you can't end a session early from within Still; iOS settings remain under your control. Please don't rely on Still where locking apps could put you or others at risk.
